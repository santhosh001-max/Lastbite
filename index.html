Overview

AsMr Foods – Giving Surplus Food a Second Chance.
AsMr Foods is an app-based food rescue platform created to reduce waste by connecting surplus food with people who need it. Instead of letting perfectly good food go to waste, food providers—including restaurants, hotels, canteens, and grocery stores—can easily share their excess food through our platform. We focus on collecting safe, high-quality meals and ingredients, making them available to the community at the lowest possible price or as a direct donation.

Our goal is to create a practical, real-time solution to food waste that makes food sharing simple, affordable, and deeply meaningful. By encouraging social responsibility, AsMr Foods helps meet local nutritional needs and protects our environment from unnecessary waste. Save Food • Share Hope • Reduce WasteDon't let good food go to waste. Join AsMr Foods today and help turn surplus into support.

About Us

Who We Are

AsMr Foods is a community-driven initiative built with a simple belief: food should be valued, not wasted. We aim to create a culture where sharing extra food becomes a meaningful way to support others and care for our environment.

Why We Started

Every day, perfectly usable food is left behind while many people struggle to access nutritious meals. AsMr Foods was created to help bridge this gap and encourage people to think differently about food waste.

What We Believe

We believe that small actions can create a big impact. By encouraging responsible food sharing and reducing unnecessary waste, we hope to build a community where no good food goes to waste and every contribution matters.

Our Vision

We are dedicated to eliminating hunger and food waste by transforming surplus resources into vital community support. Through seamless donation logistics, we turn everyday excess into meaningful nourishment, fostering a sustainable, compassionate future for all.
